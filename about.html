Hi there,
I’m Emily 🙋🏼‍♀️

I'm Emily, a UX Product Designer with a strategic focus on systems thinking, research, and solving complex problems within the healthcare industry and other highly regulated, data-sensitive environments. I specialize in translating ambiguous business and compliance challenges into scalable, intuitive digital experiences.

Currently at Raven, partnered with Collective Health, I lead product design for Claims and Internal Product areas — designing complex workflows around adjudication, EOBs, appeals/disputes, provider payments, and privacy-sensitive data access. I partner closely with Product, Engineering, and Compliance teams to navigate a highly regulated healthcare ecosystem, balancing business goals, technical constraints, and user needs.

I also own the scaling and continuous improvement of our design system, ensuring consistency, efficiency, and cohesion across interconnected platform capabilities.

Where I come from & where I’m going

I’m Portuguese, grew up in Venezuela, and currently live in Chile. This mix of cultures has allowed me to develop a broad and empathetic view of the world. I love getting to know new cultures and connecting with people from different backgrounds, which helps me better understand how they think, what motivates them, and how they interact with technology.

This global perspective enriches my approach to product design, allowing me to create more inclusive experiences aligned with the real needs of users.

I’m always excited about new experiences and opportunities that broaden my worldview, both personally and professionally.

When I’m not designing...

You’ll probably find me surrounded by plants! 🌱 I have a deep passion for nature, which led me to create La Jungla, a space where I share my love for plants, unique plant accessories, and specialty coffee. There’s something special about combining greenery with the perfect cup of coffee—it brings balance, inspiration, and a moment to slow down.

I also love traveling, discovering new coffee spots, and trying food from different cultures. Exploring new places, flavors, and traditions fuels my creativity and broadens my perspective—both in life and in design.

My creative side is always looking for something new to explore, whether it’s a skill, a craft, or a different way to bring ideas to life. Learning keeps me inspired and constantly evolving.
